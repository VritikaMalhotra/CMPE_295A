## Discussion

- Results presentation. The advisor asked the team to show the standard deviation of the measurements whenever results are presented in a bar graph, so the variability is visible alongside the reported values.
- Statistical analysis. The mixed-effects sensitivity analysis should not remain an optional, undefined step in the methodology. The team needs to decide whether it belongs in the study. If included, the workbook should explain how the analysis will be carried out and at what stage it will be used.
- Architecture. The workbook section currently called "project architecture" should be framed as "system/infrastructure architecture." Its description and diagrams need to reflect the move to the HPC environment.
- Project plan. The current plan should distinguish the work to be completed in CMPE 295A from the work planned for CMPE 295B.
- Research scope. The advisor suggested studying whether the repair approach transfers to a different domain, with computer vision as a possible example. The team has not yet decided whether to add this layer to the project.

## Decisions

- Include standard deviation when presenting measurements in bar graphs.
- Reframe the workbook architecture section around the system and HPC infrastructure.
- Separate the project plan into CMPE 295A and CMPE 295B work.
- Decide explicitly whether the mixed-effects sensitivity analysis is in scope before finalizing the methodology.

## Plans / Goals for Next Meeting

- Update the figures, architecture section, and project plan in response to the advisor's feedback.
- Bring a clear recommendation on the mixed-effects sensitivity analysis, including the method and timing if it is retained.
- Assess whether a cross-domain repair experiment is feasible and worth including.
